💌 A Letter From Mama

Dear Orion,

Happy 2nd birthday, my baby. ❤️

As I write this, you are turning two years old. You are still so little, but somehow, you have already filled my life with so many memories that I know I will carry with me forever.

One day, you will be old enough to read this letter. You might look at your two-year-old pictures and laugh at your tiny face, your little clothes, and all the things you used to do. You may not remember these days, so Mama wants to remember them for you.

At two years old, you love animals. You love looking at animal cards and books, making animal sounds, and saying “rawr” like a little lion. You love cars, buses, trains, balloons, and your picture books. You even have your own little study time. You pull Mama toward your books when you want to read, and you get upset when our little lesson is interrupted. You are always curious and eager to learn.

You may not have many words yet, but you have always found your own ways to communicate with me. You say “mama,” “papa,” “lala,” “lolo,” “tata,” “nene,” and your own little words for the things and people you love. Sometimes you call us with your little “maaa,” “taaa,” “laaaa,” or “loooo,” and Mama knows exactly what you mean.

You remember so many little things. You know where your things belong. You help clean your toys. You know the broom. You bring things to Mama. You even know little routines that I sometimes forget you are watching.

You are watching everything, my love.

You are learning from the world around you every single day, and Mama hopes that as you grow, you will always keep that curiosity and kindness inside you.

I want you to know something very important, Orion.

You have always been loved.

Even during the days when Mama was tired. Even during the days when Mama had to work. Even when I couldn't spend as much time with you as I wanted. None of those moments ever meant that I loved you less.

Mama worked because I wanted to give you a good life. I wanted you to have the things you needed, to learn, to explore, to dream, and to grow up knowing that you were worth fighting for.

If you ever wonder whether Mama loved you enough when you were little, I hope you will look at these memories and know the answer.

I loved you more than you could understand.

You were my little boy who loved books, animals, cars, and “rawr.” You were the little boy who brought things to Mama, who reached for hugs, who smiled when we clapped and praised you, and who made ordinary days feel special.

And even though you were only two, you had already taught Mama so much.

You taught me patience.
You taught me strength.
You taught me that the smallest moments can become the biggest memories.

I hope that when you read this someday, you will know that you were never a burden to Mama. You were never something that stopped my life.

You were one of the reasons I kept going.

I hope you grow up kind. I hope you respect people. I hope you are brave enough to chase what makes you happy. I hope you never feel ashamed of asking for help, and I hope you always remember where you came from.

Most of all, I hope you know that you don't have to be perfect for Mama to be proud of you.

I will be proud of you simply because you are my Orion.

Today, you are two.

But someday, you'll be ten, fifteen, twenty, or even older. You may have dreams that Mama can't even imagine yet. You may have your own friends, your own adventures, and your own life.

And that's okay.

Mama doesn't want to keep you little forever.

I just wish I could freeze this little version of you for a moment—the little boy who loves animals, books, cars, and Mama's hugs.

So if you're reading this many years from now, please remember this:

Before you were old enough to remember these days, Mama was already making memories of you.

Before you understood what love meant, you were already loved more than you could ever know.

And no matter how old you become, no matter where life takes you, you will always be my little boy.

You were loved at two.

You are loved now.

And you always will be.

Happy 2nd birthday, Orion.

Love always,

Mama ❤️
