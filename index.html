💛 A Letter From Mama

My Dearest Orion,

My dearest Orion,

You are almost two, my little boy, and sometimes Mama still cannot believe how quickly you are growing.

You are curious, clever, funny, and full of little surprises. You love animals, books, cars, buses, trains, colors, songs, and learning new things.

I love watching you discover the world one little thing at a time. I love your little words, your funny sounds, your animal noises, your smiles, and even the little things you do that make Mama laugh.

Mama works because I love you. I work because I want to give you a good life and a future full of opportunities.

If there are days when Mama is busy, please remember this: working never means loving you less.

You are loved more than you will ever know. Every little memory we make together is something Mama wants to keep forever.

One day, when you are older, I hope you open this little book and see how wonderful you were at two years old.

I hope you see how curious you were, how much you loved learning, how much you loved animals, and how much joy you brought into Mama's life.

Happy 2nd birthday, my little safari boy. 🦁🌿💛

Love always,
Mama ❤️
