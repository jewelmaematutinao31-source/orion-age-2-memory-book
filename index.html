💛 A Letter From Mama

My dearest Orion,

You are two years old now, my little explorer. Sometimes Mama looks at you and cannot believe how quickly you are growing.

I hope that someday, when you are older, you will open this little memory book and see just how loved you were at two.

I want you to remember your little words, your animal sounds, your favorite things, your funny habits, your curious little mind, and the way you loved learning and exploring the world.

You were always learning something new. You loved books, animals, colors, pictures, and your little study time with Mama.

You made Mama smile in so many little ways.

No matter how big you become, you will always be Mama's little explorer.

I love you more than words can ever explain.

Love always,
Mama 💛
