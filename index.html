💌 Mama's Letter to Orion

My dearest Orion,

You are two years old now, my little explorer. Sometimes Mama looks at you and wonders how the little baby I once held in my arms became this curious, funny, clever little boy.

I love watching you learn. I love the way you look at books, animals, colors, pictures, and everything around you with such curiosity. I love your little words, your funny sounds, your hugs, your smiles, and even your silly little habits.

You may not remember these little moments when you grow up, so Mama wanted to keep them here for you.

I hope one day, when you are older, you will open this little safari and see just how loved you were at two years old.

Keep exploring, my little boy. Keep learning. Keep being curious. And always remember that Mama loves you more than words can say.

Love always,
Mama 💛
