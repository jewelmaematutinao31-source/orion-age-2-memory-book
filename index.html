💌 A Letter From Mama

My dearest Orion,

Today you are almost two years old, and Mama still finds it hard to believe how quickly the days have passed.

You have grown from my tiny baby into this curious, clever, funny little boy who loves animals, books, cars, buses, trains, colors, songs, and learning new things.

I love watching the way you remember things, the way you point at pictures, the way you bring books to me, and the way you celebrate when you learn something new.

There are days when Mama has to work, and I wish I could spend every single moment beside you. But please always remember this: Mama works because I love you and want to give you a good life.

Working does not mean I love you any less. Every effort I make is also an expression of my love for you.

I hope that when you are older and read this, you will know just how deeply you were loved from the very beginning.

You are one of the greatest gifts of my life, Orion.

Happy 2nd birthday, my little safari boy. 🦁🌿💛

Love always,
Mama

Made with all of Mama's love for Orion. 💛🌿🦁
